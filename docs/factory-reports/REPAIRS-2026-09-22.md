## User-directed frame policy

All new seed analysis uses constant **30 fps** (`analysis-cfr30.v1`). Non-30
and variable-rate inputs are converted locally into registered, cached analysis
copies. A claimed 30 fps rate is checked against decoded frame timestamps.
The original master and any explicitly selected proxy remain immutable.
Conversion repeats/drops frames without speeding up or slowing down playback;
AAC is copied, other audio is encoded to AAC. Canvas dimensions are retained.
Ambiguous multi-stream inputs fail with an actionable error instead of silently
selecting a different track.

Standard audiovisual analysis, transcript/evidence acquisition, source-timing
repair and Flashcut's dense helper use the normalized media. Saved provenance
records both original and analysis artifact hashes. The dense helper analyzes
every frame of the **normalized copy**; it no longer promises to analyze every
native frame of a higher-rate original. This supersedes earlier native-frame
coverage wording for future runs. Original-frame indices in historical records
are not rewritten.

New blueprints and experiment outputs require **30 fps**, and quote/render
boundaries reject older non-30 output plans with a rebuild instruction. Existing
paid operations, historical evidence and finished exports are not silently
retimed or replaced. A previously analyzed non-30 seed starts a new reference
analysis revision when requested again. This code change does not retroactively
qualify the changed analysis route for live unattended use.

### Recovery scope decision

Automatic partial paid-batch recovery was removed from the backlog at the
user's request on 2026-09-22 and is no longer planned. Existing duplicate-purchase
safeguards remain: partial plans containing successful work stay paused, and
unknown provider outcomes retain their operation identity and holds. This
scope decision does not change saved attempts, charges or financial records.
