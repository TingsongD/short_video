### R01 — P1: Production reports successful rendering and delivery without doing either

**Code:** [modules/factory/production/plan.py:239](</Users/tingsongdai/Kimi-cursor/Short Form AI YouTube/modules/factory/production/plan.py:239>), [modules/factory/production/plan.py:152](</Users/tingsongdai/Kimi-cursor/Short Form AI YouTube/modules/factory/production/plan.py:152>)

### R02 — P1: Paid dispatch does not enforce the shared budget and authorization system

**Code:** [modules/factory/production/plan.py:33](</Users/tingsongdai/Kimi-cursor/Short Form AI YouTube/modules/factory/production/plan.py:33>), [modules/factory/production/plan.py:169](</Users/tingsongdai/Kimi-cursor/Short Form AI YouTube/modules/factory/production/plan.py:169>), [modules/factory/budget/service.py:86](</Users/tingsongdai/Kimi-cursor/Short Form AI YouTube/modules/factory/budget/service.py:86>), [modules/factory/discovery/service.py:32](</Users/tingsongdai/Kimi-cursor/Short Form AI YouTube/modules/factory/discovery/service.py:32>)

`ProductionService` stores `budget` but never uses it. Submission does not require an accepted experiment or authorization, reserve funds, or settle usage. **Required validation:** every paid boundary must fail closed without current scoped authority and atomic reservations; unknown effects retain holds; settlements and approved retries must remain within each native-unit and aggregate cap. Exercise these through the public run path, including research/audio/reference calls.

### R03 — P1: Editing a quoted or authorized experiment preserves stale approval

### R05 — P1: Submission is not protected against repeated calls, and real restart recovery fails

**Code:** [modules/factory/execution/executor.py:79](</Users/tingsongdai/Kimi-cursor/Short Form AI YouTube/modules/factory/execution/executor.py:79>), [modules/factory/production/plan.py:169](</Users/tingsongdai/Kimi-cursor/Short Form AI YouTube/modules/factory/production/plan.py:169>), [modules/factory/store/uow.py:122](</Users/tingsongdai/Kimi-cursor/Short Form AI YouTube/modules/factory/store/uow.py:122>), [modules/factory/execution/executor.py:116](</Users/tingsongdai/Kimi-cursor/Short Form AI YouTube/modules/factory/execution/executor.py:116>)

**Required validation:** kill a real owned worker at pre-submit, accepted-before-ack and ack-before-job-completion checkpoints. Resume the same intents without duplicate effects or terminalizing recoverable work; stale workers must be unable to submit.
