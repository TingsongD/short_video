## Acceptance seeds

One continuous-footage success does not qualify the other cases. The live
plan needs a separate source for each labeled case, or an explicit statement
that a source cannot represent that case:

1. Genuine rapid cuts, including a two-frame event.
2. Continuous footage.
3. Repeated callbacks.
4. Quiet setup and payoff.
5. Variable frame-rate timing.
6. Silence.
7. Weak rhythm, with `rhythm_unreliable` left as a valid result.
8. Clear beats.
9. Audiovisual disagreement.

For each source record the source SHA-256, profile `flashcut_hypit.v1`,
model, route, payload version, and qualification expiry. An expired or
single-seed pass does not become a general pass.

## Pass conditions

- Analysis accepts only complete, grounded responses. Truncation, schema
  rejection, real coverage gaps, and unknown HTTP outcomes stay distinct.
- Repair counters survive a restart and stop at two rounds.
- Unknown submissions are not replayed. Capacity release does not settle the
  hold.
- Narration keeps each variant's meaning, reuses unchanged speech, and derives
  captions from the final alignment.
- Editorial plans are marked provider-generated or locally constructed. The
  local plan does not invent flash cuts, callbacks, or beat drops.
- Slow footage keeps its remote operation id and is polled, not resubmitted.
- Final QC is bound to the exact artifact, revision, and policy.
- Jev stays in shadow unless a self-verifying nine-case report shows no
  mandatory loss, at least one safe optional removal, estimated downstream
  savings above Jev's cost, and p95 latency within 2 seconds.
- Reference-conditioned generation remains off until its own quoted live
  qualification passes. Embeddings are not character identity.
