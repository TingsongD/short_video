## Design decisions

- **Accepted ≠ public.** The adapter's upload response only yields a
  `request_id`; `published_at` is stamped solely from a
  provider-confirmed `public` status carrying the real post id/url.
  `draft` and `scheduled` remain visibly unpublished — a draft has no
  `remote_post_id` at all.
- **Lost ack reconciles by identity, never blind repost.** On a
  transport exception the record goes `unknown` and reconcile looks
  the submission up by `Idempotency-Key`. Same key + same payload
  replays the original request id; same key + different payload is a
  409 conflict. `retry` resubmits only after the provider confirms
  *no* remote effect (`safe_to_resubmit`).
- **Cadence before transport.** `_check_cadence` counts
  scheduled/public posts for the account on the *publication's*
  timezone day (zoneinfo, DST-correct — verified across the
  America/New_York spring-forward boundary) and blocks before a single
  byte is sent.
- **Manual lane is first-class.** `register_manual` verifies the post
  against the provider (exists, public, on the claimed account) and
  rejects mapping one post to two variants with
  `post_mapping_conflict`.
- **Deletion and metadata edits are explicit.** `delete_post` marks
  `deleted_at` on the durable record — status history is preserved,
  nothing silently vanishes; application rollback never removes posts.
- **Publication authorization is separate** from generation/delivery
  spend: the bound `Authorization` must have
  `publication_authorized=True`, be `authorized` and unexpired —
  checked again at publish time, not just at plan time.

  public/failed/unknown — **pass**.

  timezone, replay-safe — **pass**.

  **pass** (`horizon_policy` on the intent record).
- Metadata change/deletion explicit; rollback doesn't remove posts —
  **pass**.

## Open risks / manual verification

- The provider's *current* upload/status endpoint shapes are captured
  from the documented boundary; before live enablement, re-confirm
  field names (`video`, `video_url`, `platform[]`, `schedule_date`,
  `Idempotency-Key`) against live Upload Post docs (M04).
- `PUBLISHABLE` is `{youtube}` only — TikTok/Instagram stay manual
  until qualified.
- Live OAuth expiry mid-run maps to `failed` with `401` evidence;
  re-auth then `retry` is the operator path.
