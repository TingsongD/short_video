## Defects found by the drills (and fixed)

1. **`VertexAdapter` did not satisfy the executor contract.** The
   executor calls `provider.poll`; adapters exposed only `observe`.
   Fixed at the base adapter so both routes share one remote-lifecycle
   surface — no production-plan special-casing.
2. **`remote_unfinished` was treated as a permanent job failure.**
   With Vertex's single global slot, the first `RUNNING` poll failed
   the download job non-retryably, stranded its submit-capacity hold
   forever and stalled the whole plan after one interaction. Now the
   job returns to `ready` and is re-claimed; the op is re-polled until
   it reaches a terminal state.
3. **Vertex canonical requests need `model`.** The plan-level model
   was used for pricing but never injected into per-node requests; the
   fake correctly returned `model_not_found`. Takes now carry the
   model in the request payload — matching how the live adapter
   resolves it.
